## What is sound

- Small, comprehensible native architecture: Compose, immutable state, ViewModels, manual
  dependency injection and Room; no speculative service/framework layers.
- Transactional import with rechecking before mutation; explicit format-version handling;
  additive migration coverage through v7; bounded hostile-input parsing.
- A mostly pure measurement engine and explicit time/hold semantics. No additional confirmed
  mathematical defect emerged from the engine/report review; that is not a proof of correctness.
- Safety and Breathing receive narrow dependencies, making automatic risk inference difficult
  to add accidentally. External crisis actions hand off to dialer/SMS/browser rather than dial.
- Substantial deterministic and UI coverage, plus transparent historical records of unmet
  visual criteria. The five probes show why counts alone cannot establish feature completeness.

## Remaining product judgments

The current feature set is sufficient for a focused first beta once correctness checks pass.
Missing unrendered previews are lower priority than functioning recovery, preserving data,
privacy, and reachable controls. R-10 now corrects the inverted light-mode intensity direction.
The awkward 200% header wrapping, template launcher art, incomplete state-specific screenshots
and spoken TalkBack coverage remain explicit follow-ups; they are not reasons to add features.

Physical-device validation, API 26 runtime checks, signed distribution ownership, public privacy
policy/contact details and store declarations are separate release gates. This review does not
establish regulatory clearance, clinical efficacy, or suitability as a diagnostic instrument.
