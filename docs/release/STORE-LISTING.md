## App name

## Short description

Record your own day-to-day ratings and context privately on your device.

## Full description

MindScale is a private symptom journal for keeping a record you can review and discuss.
Record 0–10 ratings, sleep and wake times, notes, and marked events. Browse your history in
the Full Log, review charts in Insights, and prepare a summary to discuss with a healthcare
professional. You can also keep your own safety plan and use an optional paced-breathing tool.

Your records stay on your device. MindScale has no account, analytics, advertising, or app network
connection, and Android automatic backup is disabled. You control JSON and CSV exports and choose
where those unencrypted files are saved.

MindScale is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent a medical
condition, assess risk, or interpret your data. It is a measurement and journaling tool, not a
replacement for medical care. For medical questions, contact a healthcare professional.

The Safety card includes user-written planning space and links to external crisis resources. A
button you choose may pass information to another app. In an immediate emergency, contact local
emergency services.
