# MindScale privacy policy draft

Draft for the public policy page required before a Google Play release. This is not a published
URL.

Publisher: Kiesling Dev

Support: corykiesling@gmail.com

## What MindScale does

MindScale is a local symptom tracker. It stores records, Profile information, the safety plan,
preferences, and breathing session facts on the device where the app is installed. MindScale does
not create accounts, use analytics, connect the app to a network, or send data to a MindScale
server. Android automatic backup and device transfer of app data are disabled.

MindScale is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent a medical
condition, assess risk, or interpret a person's data. For medical questions, contact a healthcare
professional.

## Exports and sharing

The user can request JSON backups, records CSV files, clinician summaries, and selected Copy or
Share actions. Export files are unencrypted. The user chooses the document location, and that
document provider may sync the file to a cloud service. Copy places selected information on the
system clipboard, where another app or service may be able to access it. Share sends selected
information to the receiving app chosen by the user. Safety actions open the chosen dialer,
messaging app, or browser with the selected number or link. MindScale cannot control what another
app stores or sends.

Erasing data in MindScale removes it from the device. It does not remove exported files or copies
already received by another person or app.

## Data collection and retention

MindScale has no remote collection, advertising identifier, analytics event, account, or app
network service. The user controls the records kept in the app and can export or erase them from
the device. Files saved outside the app are governed by the storage provider or recipient that
holds them.

## Release details to complete
